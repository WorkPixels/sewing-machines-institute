Critical Tolerance Rules for this Lesson

  • Needle Scarf Clearance: 0.05mm – 0.10mm (about the thickness of a single sheet of notepad paper).
  • Needle Bar Rise: Bring needle to lowest dead center; raise handwheel counter-clockwise exactly 2.0mm – 2.4mm to form loop before hook tip meets scarf.
  • Lubrication: Use only synthetic mineral sewing oil. Never apply WD-40 or vegetable oils to bronze shuttle bearings.

Step-by-Step Workbench Procedure

  1. Disconnect machine from AC power and release presser foot.
  2. Remove needle plate with a short flathead technician screwdriver to prevent burring screw heads.
  3. Clean lint accumulation behind feed dogs with a natural bristle brush.
  4. Inspect hook point under direct 10x inspection light for thread grooves or needle burrs.
  5. Verify hook timing mark alignment using your SMI technician timing gauge.
